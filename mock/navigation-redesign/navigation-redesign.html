How amounts show across Home. Applies as soon as you pick one.

Shows every amount.
Discreet
Percentages, months and progress instead of amounts. Useful, but never shows how much you have.
Hidden
Masks every amount with ****.
Snapshot order
Drag to reorder the list under the hero.

HOW TO USE

  • Everything is clickable: bottom tabs, the top menu on Home, and the sliders button (opens Home Settings).
  • On Insights, the sliders button opens Insights Settings: pick which 4 numbers fill the Financial Health grid. "Show all" lists all 13 with their numbers.
  • On Insights, tap any Financial Health tile to open its detail sheet. Snapshot's "See details" jumps to Insights.
  • Pick Open, Discreet or Hidden in Home Settings. It applies immediately; the sliders button shows a small badge while Discreet or Hidden is on.

STRUCTURE

  • Bottom bar: Home · Insights · Record · Profile · Learn. Investment and History stop being tabs; they move into Home's top menu as Holdings and Ledger.
  • Home top menu: Snapshot · Ledger · Holdings · Goals · Loans. The header (greeting, settings) stays put; the menu sticks under it when scrolling.
  • Home Settings: privacy style + Snapshot row order. Lives here because every money view now sits under Home.
  • Insights: trends over time — spending, budgets, the Financial Health section and the AI chat entry.
  • Financial Health grid replaces the old horizontally scrolling overview cards: a fixed 2×2 grid, no swipe. The user picks its 4 numbers from 13 (the 10 old overview cards + Spending pace, Debt drag, Investment boost).
  • Financial Health screen is removed. Its four cards become bottom sheets opened from the Insights tiles: Runway → Financial State, Spending pace → Spending & Cash Flow, Debt drag → Debt Pressure, Investment boost → Wealth Growth. "How is this calculated?" is a collapsible inside each sheet (no modal on a modal).
  • Profile: only account and app settings — data, backup, security, appearance, intelligence.
  • Learn: Help Center guides, Math & Formulas, and everything about WealthSnap (why free, manifesto, contact, rate).

NOT MOCKED

  • Floating gear bubble — its per-screen actions need rethinking once tabs change.
  • Dark mode and empty states.
