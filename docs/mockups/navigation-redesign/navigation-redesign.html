How to use

  • Everything is clickable: bottom tabs, Home's underline tabs, rows, tiles, "Overall ⌄", "See all", "Show all", and the sliders buttons (Home Settings / Insights Settings).
  • Light / Dark above the phone switches theme. Privacy (Open / Discreet / Hidden) is in Home Settings.
  • On Insights, tap a Financial Health tile for its detail sheet; Insights Settings picks and orders the 4 tiles.

Visual rules (docs/DESIGN.md)

  • Content sits on the page. Lists are hairline rows, never boxed. Only tiles (inset fill) and sheets (floating) are containers.
  • Accent marks only "tappable" or "you are here". The hero is a number, not a colored block.
  • Domain color = an 8px dot or a progress fill. Never text, icon circle or background.
  • Green / red mean good / bad, not up / down (debt going down is green).
  • One font (Inter), five sizes, tabular figures for every number.
