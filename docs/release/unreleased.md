## 🏠 Home Snapshot
- **One calm overview instead of five loud cards**: Home used to stack five full-color cards (Financial Health, Cash Flow, Portfolio, Debts, Savings Goals), each with its own big number, button and two or three swipe pages, which pushed your recent transactions far down the screen. Home now opens on a Snapshot: your Projected Net Worth as the one big number, how much it moved this month ("▲ ₱15,100 · 3.2% this month"), a small line showing your past months (six by default), and your Runway and Total Assets underneath. "Details" opens Insights.
- **Four rows, one switch**: Cash, Investments, Loans and Goals each get one row with a short context line (money in and out, unrealized gain, what's due this month, % of target) and how it changed since last month. One "Overall / This month" switch above them replaces every card's swipe pages: in This month, Cash shows Income − Expense with transfers on the line below, and Loans shows how much of this month's minimum payments you've made. Tap a row to jump to its tab.
- **Recent transactions up front**: Your three latest transactions sit right under the rows, with "See all" opening the Ledger tab.
- **Home settings follow the tab you're on**: The sliders button opens a sheet named for the selected tab. Every tab has the privacy mode. Snapshot adds how far back the net worth graph reaches (6 months, 1 year, 3 years or all time) and the order of its rows (net worth stays on top); Holdings adds the order of its sections (Holdings, Allocation, Dividends, Smart advisor). Your old card order carries over automatically.
- **Everything else is one tab away**: A row of tabs under the greeting (Snapshot · Ledger · Holdings · Goals · Loans) stays in place while you scroll and opens the full view of each:
  - **Ledger**: search, All / Income / Expense / Transfers filters, this month's money in (income + transfers in) and out (expenses + transfers out), and every transaction grouped by day with the day's total. Tap the calendar link for the month calendar. Tapping a transaction opens the same edit and delete options as History. Investments and loans recorded without a cash entry still appear in the list, tagged "No cash entry". Under In / Out, **Safe to spend** shows what's left for the rest of the month after recurring bills, unpaid debt minimums, goal contributions and your usual daily living costs (tap it for the breakdown). It's the monthly figure History showed; History's Daily, Weekly and Yearly views are gone. Day headers from earlier years now include the year.
  - **Holdings**: your portfolio value and total return (unrealized gains, realized gains and dividends over everything you ever invested), with realized and dividends underneath, a bar showing how it splits across stocks, funds, crypto and other types, and each holding with its value, weight and return. Tap a holding for its trades, price and dividend history. The allocation treemap, your dividends (Actual, Calendar, Projected) and Smart Advisor all sit right on the page, each with a header link that switches its view; "Add an investment" and "Fetch price list" sit under Tools, and "Fetch price list" now goes straight to its Today / Last 3 days / Last 7 days options instead of a settings menu.
  - **Goals**: what you've saved in goals, this month's contributions, and one progress row per goal with whether it's on track. "New goal" and tapping a goal (add funds, withdraw, pause, edit, delete) work as before. This replaces the Savings Goals screen.
  - **Loans**: switch between "You owe" and "Owed to you", see what's outstanding and still due (or still expected) this month, and one progress row per loan with its next due date. Tap a loan to record a payment, edit it or mark it paid off. Your payoff plan (debt-free date, Avalanche / Snowball, interest still to pay) and paid-off loans sit below. This replaces the Debt Strategy screen.
- **Obligations count each loan separately (fix)**: Home's "₱3.1k of ₱4.5k due this month" used to add up every payment you made, so paying extra on one loan made another loan's unpaid minimum look covered. It now counts each loan's payment only up to its own minimum.
- **Net worth history and paid-off loans**: A loan you mark Paid off or Forgiven counts in past net worth up to the day you marked it, then drops out, so its payoff shows once in that month's change.

## 📊 Insights: Financial Health grid
- **Four numbers, your pick**: Insights used to open on a swipeable row of 10 cards, two at a time, while Runway's deeper story, spending pace, debt drag and investment boost lived on a separate Financial Health screen. Insights now opens on a fixed 2×2 grid. You choose which 4 of 13 numbers it shows: Runway, Spending pace, Debt drag, Investment boost, Budget health, Net cash flow, Savings rate, Total income, Total expense, Burn rate, Daily average, Annualized expense and Top category. It starts with Runway, Spending pace, Debt drag and Investment boost.
- **Pick and order them in Insights settings**: The sliders button opens a checklist with an "N of 4" counter. The order you check numbers in is their order on the grid (Card 1–4); unchecking one moves the ones after it up, and once 4 are checked the rest wait until you uncheck one. The same sheet still lets you drag the sections below the grid into your preferred order, which carries over. The old "reorder the summary cards" option is gone, and the grid starts from the default four.
- **Show all**: The link next to "Financial health" lists all 13 numbers with their current values, with the ones on your grid tagged by card number.
- **A sheet behind every number**: Tap a tile or row for its details. Runway, Spending pace, Debt drag and Investment boost carry everything the Financial Health screen showed: when your cash would run out, how spending, debts and investments move your runway, net flow and its impact on self-sustain, mandatory payments, interest cost and self-sustain delay, the "If you add extra per month" stepper (now showing how many months sooner you'd be debt-free), and what investing your surplus would do. The other nine show their value with a short formula. Every sheet has a collapsible "How is this calculated?", including the old Runway, Budget Health and Daily Average info popups and the Financial Health help, so nothing opens on top of a sheet.
- **Budgets and Ask**: A Budgets section counts how many budgets are over, close to the limit and on track, then shows your five most-used budgets with their % used this month, colored like Budget Health; "Show all" opens every budget in a sheet. "Ask about your money" opens the AI chat.
- **Financial Health screen removed**: Its content lives in the Insights sheets above.
- **Investment boost's rate is labeled honestly**: The figure the old Wealth Growth card called "12-Month Return" is your dividend yield (or an assumed 7.2% when there's no dividend data), so the sheet now calls it "Dividend yield" or "Assumed return".
- **Runway and Budget Health tiles**: Runway below zero cash now shows the same negative figure as Home instead of 0.0 months, and Budget Health shows 0% (under budget) instead of "N/A" when you have budgets but haven't spent against them yet.
- **No pull-to-refresh**: Insights reloads each time you open it, so the pull-down gesture is gone.

## 🧭 New Navigation: Five Tabs
- **A bottom bar built around the redesign**: The bar is now Home · Insights · Record · Profile · Learn. Insights is a tab of its own instead of a screen behind Home, so it's one tap from anywhere; the Snapshot's "Details" link opens it too. Record stays the raised button in the middle.
- **Investment and History tabs are gone**: Everything they showed lives in Home's Holdings and Ledger tabs.
- **Profile is just settings**: Profile now opens on your name and currency, then four short groups: Money setup (Budgets, Recurring rules, Reminders, Asset dictionary), Data (Auto backup, Import / export), Security and intelligence (App lock, Google Gemini) and Appearance (Theme). Auto backup and Import / export (backup and restore files, CSV import, Excel export, device sync, Clear all data) each open their own screen. Theme opens a small sheet instead of three always-visible buttons.
- **New Learn tab**: Guides, help and app info used to sit at the bottom of Profile. They now have their own tab: a shelf of guides (Getting Started, Home Snapshot, What Runway Tells You, Privacy Modes, Saving with Goals, Financial Insights, Debt Strategy, Investments), a Help center (Math and formulas, a new FAQ, Contact the developer, feedback, Terms of use and privacy) and About WealthSnap (Why it's free, the manifesto, the developer's message, the supporter wall, support links, Rate WealthSnap, and the version, which opens the change log).

## 🕶️ Privacy Modes: Open, Discreet, Hidden
- **Discreet keeps Home useful in public**: The old privacy toggle replaced every amount with `****`, which left Home blank and still told anyone looking over your shoulder there was something worth hiding. Discreet shows only numbers that reveal no amounts: your net worth's % change this month, savings rate, Runway in months, cash kept as a % of everything you've earned, investment return %, % of loans repaid and payments made, % to your goals and how many are on track, with progress bars. Single transactions still show their amount, since one purchase says nothing about your totals.
- **Hidden is today's privacy mode**: Every amount shows as `****`, keeping a leading + or − sign. If you had privacy turned on, you're in Hidden after the update; if it was off, you're in Open.
- **Every Home tab has a Discreet version**: Ledger shows each transaction's amount but hides day totals, and shows this month's Out as a % of In; Holdings shows total return, realized and dividend %, top holding, and each holding's weight and return; Goals shows % to target and how many goals got a contribution this month; Loans shows % repaid (or % collected for money owed to you), how many of this month's payments were made, and the payoff plan's interest as a % of what you owe.
- **Insights has a Discreet version too**: The Financial Health grid and its sheets show each number's scale-free version: Runway, Spending pace, Debt drag, Budget health and Savings rate as they are; net cash flow as the % of income kept; total expense as a % of income; total income vs your average; burn rate and annualized expense as a % of your average income; daily average vs your usual daily spending; the top category's share of spending. Investment boost shows "—", since a large figure would reveal how much you've invested, and the Debt drag stepper counts steps from zero instead of amounts (it doesn't start from the income-based suggestion, which would give your income away). Budgets show % only. Ledger's Safe to spend shows "—".
- **No more floating gear**: The settings icon on Home and Insights is now a plain button you can't drag out. Monthly summary moved to Profile → Data, and "Reveal for screenshot" to Profile → Security (shown in Open and Discreet).
- **Close with the X**: Info sheets (Contact the developer, Support, Why it's free, the developer's message, Manifesto) close with the X in their header instead of a Close / Got it button, like every other sheet.
- **Detail sheets follow the mode**: The options sheets that open when you tap a transaction, investment or loan used to show real amounts even with privacy on. They now show `****` in Hidden (and the amount in Discreet, like the row you tapped); a holding's trade and price history masks amounts in Discreet too.
- **Charts in Discreet**: Insights and Holdings charts stay on screen in Discreet, drawn without amounts: axes drop their amounts and tooltips show percentages (vs your average, share of spending or of the year). Hidden replaces each chart with the same lock placeholder everywhere.
- **Screenshots**: Open and Discreet block screenshots; Hidden allows them. Profile → Security → "Reveal for screenshot" allows one for 15 seconds in Open or Discreet.
- **Applies everywhere**: Screens that don't have a Discreet design yet show Hidden while Discreet is on. Budgets, Recurring rules and Smart suggestions used to show their amounts with privacy on and now mask them; Monthly summary, a written recap full of amounts, asks you to switch to Open to read it. The old eye buttons are gone, since privacy now lives in Home settings.
- **New Help Center guides**: "Home Snapshot" explains every number on the Snapshot. "What Runway Tells You" covers how runway and burn rate are worked out and what moves them, "Privacy Modes" what each mode shows and how screenshots behave, and "Saving with Goals" how goals affect your numbers and what on track means. A new FAQ answers common questions about your data, backups, Gemini and screenshots. "Financial Insights" and "Math & Formulas" now cover the Financial Health grid, its four detail sheets and each number's Discreet version.

## 🎨 A New Look Everywhere
- **Layered, with a little colour**: Screens now sit on a soft grey canvas, with each section in its own white rounded group, so lists, charts and forms read as distinct blocks instead of one flat white page. The main number on each screen sits in a light blue panel, the net worth line has a faint shade under it, and rows show their icon in a small tile tinted with the colour of Cash, Investments, Loans or Goals. Dark mode follows the same layers.
- **Sheets and pop-ups**: Every bottom sheet uses the new design: a grab handle, a plain title with an X, rows separated by thin lines instead of boxed cards, and one clear action button in a footer band where the sheet has one.
- **Record forms**: Expense, income, transfer, investment, loan, savings goal and receipt review forms use the same layout: a type switch instead of red and green blocks, a large amount with no box, grey input fields, tiles for categories and destinations, and a footer with Cancel and a button that says what it saves ("Save expense", "Save transfer", "Save loan").
- **View pickers drop down**: Links like "Overall ⌄", "By value ⌄" or "6M ⌄" open a small list right under the link, with a check on the current choice. The 6M / 1Y / 3Y / All picker on Insights charts used to open a centered pop-up.
- **Charts**: Bars show recorded amounts in solid blue and the projected rest of this month in a light tint. Monthly pulse shades the area under what you've actually spent.
- **Getting Started**: The guide opens full screen from Learn instead of inside a sheet, so its slides aren't cut off.
